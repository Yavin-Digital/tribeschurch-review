    Worth noticing

    • Youth Connect Card has 168 submissions and no automation at all.
    • No form adds people to a list or sets a field.
    • The Connect Card's checkboxes for baptism, deciding to follow Jesus, and joining a Tribe do not feed any workflow.

    What is automated, and what is done by hand

    Automated today

    • Connect Card and TribesKids Check-in Form add people to First Time Guest, skipping anyone already started or completed.
    • Benevolence Request adds people to Benevolence.
    • Two forms add people to the hidden workflow.
    • Seven forms send an email on submission.

    Done by hand

    • Every workflow step: each one is assigned to a person.
    • The welcome email is typed by hand.
    • Both welcome texts are sent from staff phones.
    • The welcome card is handwritten.
    • Cards for Be Baptized, I Have Decided Card and Interest in Joining a Tribe are added by hand.

    The gaps we found

    1. Three workflows have no way in. Be Baptized, I Have Decided Card and Interest in Joining a Tribe only get cards when someone adds them.
    2. Connect Card checkboxes go nowhere. Baptism, deciding to follow Jesus, and joining a Tribe answers do not start any follow-up.
    3. A workflow we could not see. Plan Your Visit (39) and TribesYouth Intern Application (59) feed it. Only its creator can grant access.
    4. No step automations. No workflow step sends an email or acts on its own.
    5. Lists drive nothing. None of the 8 lists feeds a workflow. Three have auto-refresh turned off, two flagged as needing attention.
    6. Youth Connect Card is unwired. 168 submissions, no workflow, no email.
    7. No other triggers. Form automations are the only thing that starts a workflow.

    Scope: only the 5 active workflows were reviewed. Archived or inactive workflows and snoozed cards were not.

    Section 2

    Planning Center vs Subsplash

    Could Subsplash Workflows do this job better? What would it take to move?

    Recommendation

    Move only if the church commits to Subsplash One

    Subsplash can take over all five follow-up workflows. Make the move only as part of a church-wide decision to run people care in Subsplash One with Church Management, not as a feature upgrade on its own.

    Subsplash is not more capable here

    Planning Center's 2026 releases can close the same gaps today, at no cost, without moving.

    The real case for Subsplash

    Consolidation: app, giving, people and workflows in one dashboard. Not better workflow features.

    Side by side

    What each platform's official documentation says.

    FeaturePlanning CenterSubsplash
    A form creates a cardYesYes
    Start only on a specific answerYesYes; checkbox matching not documented
    Several forms into one workflowYesNo, one form per workflow

    What would change, and what would not

    Subsplash would automate

    • The welcome email, as an automatic step email.
    • Starting baptism, decided and join-a-Tribe follow-up from Connect Card answers, once tested.
    • The Join a Tribe initial email.
    • The Benevolence "request received" email.
    • Step assignees and due dates.

    Stays manual either way

    • The handwritten welcome card.
    • Welcome texts. Personal texts from a staff phone remain the warmest option.
    • Benevolence decisions and conversations, by design.
    • Reviewing guest details and connecting guests to staff and serve team leaders.
    • Noting the Benevolence category on a profile.

    Limits to know about Subsplash

    • One form per workflow. First Time Guest is fed by two forms today, so the kids check-in form needs another approach.
    • Emails cannot be delayed. They send the moment a card enters a step.
    • Texts are a manual send outside workflows, and need Subsplash One with Church Management.
    • Subsplash Forms cannot update existing profiles or custom fields.
    • The Planning Center integration is about giving. It syncs name, address and birth date one way, and does not move workflows. People data should come over by spreadsheet (CSV) import.

    A fair note on Planning Center

    If Tribes stays, the same gaps can be closed in an afternoon, at no cost:

    1. Add Connect Card automations for the three checkboxes so baptism, decided and join-a-Tribe answers start their follow-up.
    2. Make the Welcome E-Mail step send automatically, with an after-hours hold (auto snooze).
    3. Automate the Interest in Joining a Tribe initial email.

    Planning Center can also keep two forms feeding First Time Guest, which Subsplash cannot.

    If the church moves: the seven-phase path

    Week ranges are estimates and assume the Subsplash account is active. Nothing in Planning Center is switched off until Phase 6.

    1. 1

      Prerequisites

      Week 1

      Confirm Subsplash One with Church Management, fix the login, name an Admin, assign Workflow Manager roles.

    2. 2

      People data

      Weeks 1-2

      Export from Planning Center to CSV, keep a backup, clean it, import, merge duplicates, spot-check.

    3. 3

      Rebuild the forms

      Week 2

      Connect Card, TribesKids Check-in and Benevolence Request, with separate first visit, baptism, decided and Tribe questions.

    4. 4

      Build the workflows

      Weeks 2-3

      Steps, assignees, due dates and checklists. Connect forms and confirm who can see Benevolence.

    5. 5

      Emails, testing, parallel run

      Weeks 3-6

      Write step emails, test with staff submissions, then run both systems for 2 to 4 Sundays.

    6. 6

      Cut over

      Week 6 or 7

      Point every link and QR code to Subsplash, close Planning Center forms, let open cards finish there.

    7. 7

      Retire Planning Center workflows

      When open cards finish

      Record history, archive the five workflows, map and rebuild the hidden workflow.

    Risks to plan for

    Kids form cannot feed First Time Guest

    Launch a separate kids workflow; review after 60 days.

    Checkbox fix could fail

    Test before cutover; fall back to manual cards.

    Emails send instantly

    Put emails on steps entered after a staff action.

    Guests can unsubscribe

    Keep key touches personal: calls, texts, the card.

    Benevolence visibility

    Confirm who can see these sensitive requests before go-live.

    Two systems at once

    One entry point for Subsplash and a weekly check-in.

    Missing emails or duplicates

    CSV import, error review and duplicate merge.

    Cost unknown

    Get a Subsplash One quote before Phase 2.

    Open questions for Tribes Church

    1. Has Tribes purchased Subsplash One with Church Management, and who is the account Admin?
    2. Will Planning Center stay as the people or giving system, or be retired entirely? Does Tribes use Planning Center Giving?
    3. Who owns the hidden workflow, and what are its steps?
    4. Should the welcome email go out instantly, or after a staff review (the default proposed)?
    5. Kids check-in: merge into the Connect Card, or run a separate kids workflow? Is Subsplash check-in planned?
    6. Who should be able to see Benevolence cards?
    7. Will staff keep texting from their phones or use Subsplash SMS? The Consent to Text form has 0 submissions.
    8. What do the current welcome email and Benevolence Request email say, and who receives the latter?

    Not yet confirmed

    Treat these as unknown until tested or confirmed by the vendor.

    • Whether a Subsplash form can start a workflow from a checkbox answer.
    • One Subsplash form feeding several workflows: on the product page, not in the how-to guide.
    • Whether Subsplash can skip duplicates, branch, auto-advance after an email, or let staff add cards by hand.
    • Per-workflow privacy in Subsplash, which matters for Benevolence.
    • Texting as a workflow step on either platform.
    • Creating Subsplash cards through Zapier or the API.
    • Subsplash One pricing and text message limits for Tribes' package.
    • Tribes' own Subsplash account: not set up yet, and the dashboard login did not work.
    • The hidden Planning Center workflow's name, steps and owner.
